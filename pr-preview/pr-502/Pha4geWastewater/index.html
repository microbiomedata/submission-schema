    Subset: Pha4geWastewater

    The slots and enums aligned to the PHA4GE Wastewater Contextual Data Specification 4.4.4, so that NMDC records and PHA4GE wastewater surveillance records can be crosswalked.

    URI: Pha4geWastewater

    Comments

    • The first see_also link is the specification release the alignment was made against. The second is the DataHarmonizer template (cidgoh/pathogen-genomics-package tag PGPv5.6.5, templates/wastewater/schema.yaml) whose slot_uris supplied the GENEPIO ids in the members' close_mappings and related_mappings. When PHA4GE releases a new version, update the version and the two links here; the members carry no copy of them.
    • Members are NMDC-native slots and enums in src/schema/core.yaml, basic_classes.yaml and external_identifiers.yaml, which carry in_subset in their own definitions, and MIxS slots in the generated src/schema/mixs.yaml, whose membership is asserted in assets/yq-for-mixs-customizations.txt after the build strips the upstream MIxS subsets.
    • Each member's notes say which PHA4GE field it maps to and why the predicate is close or related. No member uses exact_mappings, even where the shapes agree.
